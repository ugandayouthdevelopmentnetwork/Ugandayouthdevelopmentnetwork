01

About UYDNet

Our mission

To connect, equip and transform young people and communities through collaborative action, capacity building and practical support.

Vision

A stronger, healthier and more empowered youth population contributing to a better Uganda.

UYDNet emerged from the realities affecting young people and communities — limited access to skills, weak participation, poor coordination and recurring unmet needs.
