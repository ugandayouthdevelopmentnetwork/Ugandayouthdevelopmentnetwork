Overview

Uganda Youth Development Network Organization (UYDNet) promotes youth development, leadership, participation, skills, community wellbeing and institutional action across Uganda.

About UYDNet

About UYDNet & the Founder’s Story

From everyday observation to organised action

UYDNet emerged from a strong concern for the realities affecting young people and communities, including limited access to skills, weak participation, poor coordination and recurring unmet needs.

The organisation was founded to respond practically through partnerships, community engagement and youth-centred programmes.

Founding principle

The Founder establishes the vision; the institution carries it forward. Organisational funds, property and assets belong to UYDNet, not personal ownership.

Rules & Regulations

Rules & Regulations

The organisation operates through a framework of governance, responsibility, cooperation and accountability. These rules guide the internal functions, leadership, participation and implementation of programmes.

  • Members participate in leadership, projects and community action.
  • Programs are guided by cooperation, accountability and shared purpose.
  • Strategic action supports youth empowerment and community wellbeing.
